# Uncertainty → Credit

An interactive evidence atlas for:

> Angela, I. T. (2026). “Global economic policy uncertainty and credit risk in emerging economies: evidence from Bangladesh.” *Future Business Journal*, 12:99.

The dashboard reorganises the article’s published figures, tables, estimates and policy conclusions into an interactive static website. It introduces no new observations or empirical model.

## Evidence represented

- Published NPL and GEPU time-series figure, 2000–2021
- Baseline coefficient estimates and HAC standard errors across five specifications
- Crisis interactions and crisis-period dummies
- Alternative HAC bandwidths and pairs-bootstrap inference
- Rolling-window stability and sequential crisis-year exclusions
- Alternative GEPU transformations and lag structures
- Unit-root classifications, model diagnostics, limitations and policy implications
